## 1. Super Admin Module (Platform Owner)

The **Super Admin** is the highest-level administrator of the **Multi-Tenant SaaS Gym Management System**. The Super Admin manages the **entire platform**, including all registered gyms, subscriptions, analytics, revenue, security, and communication.

### Responsibilities

The Super Admin can:

* Register and manage multiple gyms
* Approve, suspend, or deactivate gyms
* Manage subscription plans
* Monitor platform-wide analytics
* Revenue tracking
* Configure system settings
* Manage user roles & permissions
* Handle security and backups
* View performance of all gyms
* Send announcements to gym owners

Every gym record must be isolated using:

```sql

```

with **Supabase Row Level Security (RLS)** enabled to ensure data separation across gyms.
